Privacy Policy – SetPilot

SetPilot remembers the last time you lifted and logs this session on this phone. Routines, sets, coins, and shop unlocks stay on the device. We do not create an account or upload a workout.

What stays on the device

  • Routines, logged sets, and rest presets
  • Coin balance and shop unlocks
  • Purchase tokens needed to restore Remove ads and SetPilot Pro

Services

  • Google Play Billing – coin packs, Remove ads, and SetPilot Pro (handled by Google)
  • Remote config – GET https://api2.blwsmartware.net/N316.json to read the disable field (no personal data is sent)
  • Export – a file is shared only when you tap Export

SetPilot does not coach, track food, or post to a social feed. Contact the developer through the Play Store listing.
